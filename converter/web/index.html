GT2 PSP
Converter

Turns your own Gran Turismo 2 discs into a native PSP version. Everything is built here, on your computer, from your discs - nothing of the game is downloaded.
1Your discs

Put the .cue and .bin files into input/ and press Search again. Supported: the USA discs - Simulation Mode (Rev 2) and Arcade Mode. One disc is enough; with both you get a start menu to pick the mode.

Simulation Mode

GT mode, licenses, career - the main disc
No disc selected

Arcade Mode

Road race, rally, time trial - and the intro movie
No disc selected
